ThineCraft project scope

ShowCraft

Status: Placeholder project. This document describes intended direction; it does not claim that working functionality exists.

Project website

Purpose

ShowCraft is the planned open environment for diagrams, animation, business intelligence, and digital twins. It is intended to bridge explanation and observation with one visual language.

Confirmed direction

  • Diagrams with shapes, connectors, layers, data, semantics, and reusable visual vocabularies.
  • Models that are computable as well as readable.
  • Timelines, transitions, animation, and presentation controls for explaining behavior over time.
  • Data connections that can turn an explanatory model into a live operational view.
  • Business-intelligence views and digital-twin representations without abandoning the underlying visual structure.

Boundaries and non-goals

  • ShowCraft is broader than slide presentation software; diagrams, data, animation, and live models are central.
  • It should preserve meaning in a model rather than reducing every view to decorative graphics.
  • Source records remain in their originating systems; ShowCraft presents and relates them.
  • No implementation, rendering engine, connector, or production readiness is currently promised.

Expected integrations

  • ShareCraft for governed models, pages, records, and publishing.
  • GanttCraft and FlowCraft for process and schedule visualization.
  • TeamCraft for presentation and collaborative explanation.
  • SolidCraft for 3D models that participate in explanatory or operational views.
  • External data sources through open, inspectable connectors.

Open decisions

  • Document and semantic model for shapes, data, behaviors, layers, and reusable vocabularies.
  • 2D, 3D, animation, rendering, collaboration, and versioning architecture.
  • Data refresh, calculations, historical state, alerts, and digital-twin synchronization.
  • Export, embedding, accessibility, and offline presentation formats.

Guidance for AI contributors

  1. Treat diagrams, animation, BI, and digital twins as one connected scope.
  2. Do not describe planned visual or data capabilities as implemented.
  3. Preserve semantic meaning, portability, and inspectable data connections.
  4. Do not silently copy ownership of source records into ShowCraft.
  5. Record new product decisions in this file before presenting them as settled scope.
