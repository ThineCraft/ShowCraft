The proposition

Built to remain understandable—and yours.

ShowCraft is intended to bridge explanation and observation: the same visual language can describe an idea, animate a process, reveal live measures, or represent a changing system.

Proposed scope

What ShowCraft is intended to make possible.

01

Planned capability

Diagrams with meaning

Shapes, connectors, layers, data, and reusable visual vocabularies are intended to make diagrams computable as well as readable.

02

Planned capability

Animation and narrative

Timelines, transitions, and presentation controls are planned for explaining how a system behaves over time.

03

Planned capability

BI and digital twins

Data connections are intended to turn a model into a live operational view without abandoning its explanatory structure.
